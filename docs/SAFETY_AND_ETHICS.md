# Safety, Privacy, and Responsible-Use Statement

## Synthetic Data
Every user, target, device, IP cluster, session, content record, and investigative event is fictional.
No real platform information, personal data, or proprietary data is used.

## Harm Minimization
Content is represented through safe analytical placeholders. The repository does not reproduce
graphic material, extremist propaganda, sexual content, child-safety material, or operational
instructions for violence, evasion, or abuse.

## Human Accountability
Automated queries, scores, and network links are investigative leads. Human review is required for:

- evidence validation
- contextual interpretation
- credibility assessments
- enforcement recommendations
- public-safety escalation
- legal or policy conclusions

## False Positives
The portfolio includes benign and mixed-ground-truth cases to demonstrate that coordination,
similarity, or high activity alone does not establish harmful intent.

## Independence
This is an independent professional portfolio project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.
